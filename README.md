# Merchant Simulator

**A third-person shop management simulation game built with Unreal Engine 5.**

**Status:** Early Development / Planning  
**Engine:** Unreal Engine 5  
**Development:** Blueprint Visual Scripting  
**Developer:** Jalyn Figueroa-Kelley

## Overview

Merchant Simulator is a personal game development project centered around building and managing a small retail business.

Players will start with a limited amount of money and an empty shop. They'll be responsible for purchasing inventory, stocking shelves, setting prices, and selling products to customers. As their business becomes more profitable, they'll have opportunities to expand their store and improve its operations.

The goal is to create a game that combines business management with an interactive third-person experience, where players can see how their decisions affect the growth of their shop.

This project is also an opportunity for me to improve my Unreal Engine skills while exploring different areas of software development, including gameplay programming, product design, and software testing.

## Inspiration

The idea for Merchant Simulator comes partly from my experience working in restaurant management.

I've spent time managing inventory, coordinating employees, and making decisions that affect daily business operations. Those experiences made me interested in how similar systems could be translated into gameplay mechanics.

I want to create a game where managing a business feels rewarding, but where players also have to think about how they spend their money, what products they stock, and how they grow their store.

## Planned Gameplay Features

### Inventory Management
- Purchase products from suppliers.
- Track available inventory and product quantities.
- Stock products on shelves.
- Automatically update inventory after sales.
- Prevent transactions when inventory is insufficient.

### Business and Economy
- Begin with a limited amount of starting capital.
- Set selling prices for products.
- Track revenue, expenses, and profits.
- Reinvest earnings into additional inventory.
- Balance product costs against potential profits.

### Third-Person Gameplay
- Explore and manage a shop using a third-person character.
- Interact with shelves, counters, and supplier terminals.
- Purchase and organize products.
- Complete customer transactions.

### Future Expansion
Features being considered for later development include:
- Customer AI and purchasing behavior.
- Store upgrades and customization.
- Employee hiring and management.
- Additional product categories.
- More advanced economic systems.

These features are planned concepts and have not yet been implemented.

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

My first development goal is to create a small but functional gameplay loop:

1. Start with a fixed amount of money.
2. Purchase products from a supplier.
3. Add purchased products to inventory.
4. Stock products in the shop.
5. Sell products and receive payment.
6. Use the profits to purchase more inventory.

The focus of this first version will be functionality rather than graphics or complex gameplay systems.

Once the core mechanics are working, I'll begin improving the interface, balancing the economy, and expanding the available interactions.

## Testing and Quality Assurance

I plan to incorporate testing throughout development rather than waiting until the game is finished.

Areas I intend to test include:

- Inventory quantities updating correctly.
- Purchases deducting the correct amount of money.
- Sales increasing the player's balance.
- Transactions being rejected when funds or inventory are insufficient.
- Invalid inputs not producing unexpected results.
- Existing gameplay features continuing to work after changes.

As development progresses, I intend to document test cases, track bugs, and explore automated testing within Unreal Engine.

Test results and completed testing work will be added to this repository as they become available.
